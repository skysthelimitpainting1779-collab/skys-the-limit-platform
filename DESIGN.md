# Sky's the Limit Painting LLC — Brand Design System

## Visual Direction
- **Identity**: Premium, owner-led local painting contractor for Residential, Commercial, and Public-Sector projects.
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, prep-first, clean execution, safety-conscious.

## Color Palette Tokens
- **Primary Accent**: Established Sky's Orange (`#E65100` / `hsl(21, 100%, 45%)`)
- **Base Surfaces**: Charcoal Gray (`#1E293B`), Warm White (`#FAFAFA`), Neutral Light Gray (`#F1F5F9`)
- **Text & Contrast**: High-contrast Charcoal (`#0F172A`) on Light, White on Dark (WCAG 2.2 AA compliant).

## Typography & Geometry
- **Font**: Inter / Modern System Sans (high legibility).
- **Geometry**: Strong, practical rounded corners (`rounded-lg` / `8px`).

## Photography Policy
- Authentic owner and project photos only. Generic AI contractor stock photos are prohibited.
